# AUTHORITIES — Legal Research

## Constitutional Provisions
- **Fourth Amendment** — Unreasonable seizures, searches, arrests
- **Fourteenth Amendment** — Due process, equal protection
- **42 U.S.C. § 1983** — Civil action for deprivation of rights

## Key Case Law

### Unlawful Seizure / Traffic Stop
- *Terry v. Ohio*, 392 U.S. 1 (1968) — reasonable suspicion standard
- *Whren v. United States*, 517 U.S. 806 (1996) — traffic stops and probable cause
- *Navarette v. California*, 572 U.S. 393 (2014) — reasonable suspicion for traffic stop
- *Arizona v. Johnson*, 555 U.S. 323 (2009) — traffic stop scope and duration

### Unlawful Arrest
- *Tennessee v. Garner*, 471 U.S. 1 (1985) — seizure of fleeing felons
- *Graham v. Connor*, 490 U.S. 386 (1989) — objective reasonableness standard
- *Michigan v. Summers*, 452 U.S. 692 (1981) — detention incidental to search
- *Bailey v. United States*, 568 U.S. 186 (2013) — detention scope

### Excessive Force
- *Graham v. Connor*, 490 U.S. 386 (1989) — objective reasonableness
- *Kingsley v. Hendrickson*, 576 U.S. 389 (2015) — objective reasonableness for pre-trial detainees (14th Amend.)
- *Hudson v. McMillian*, 503 U.S. 1 (1992) — excessive force even without serious injury
- *Scott v. Harris*, 550 U.S. 372 (2007) — video evidence overrides account

### Brady / Exculpatory Evidence
- *Brady v. Maryland*, 373 U.S. 83 (1963) — suppression of exculpatory evidence
- *Giglio v. United States*, 405 U.S. 150 (1972) — impeachment evidence = Brady material
- *Kyles v. Whitley*, 514 U.S. 419 (1995) — prosecution's duty to disclose
- *Smith v. Cain*, 565 U.S. 73 (2012) — reasonable probability standard

### Malicious Prosecution
- *Castellano v. Fragozo*, 352 F.3d 939 (5th Cir. 2003) — Fourth Amendment malicious prosecution
- *Hewitt v. City of Gretna*, 2012 WL 1648320 (E.D. La. 2012) — elements of malicious prosecution
- *United States v. Armitage*, 2023 WL (5th Cir.) — prosecution without probable cause

### Monell Municipal Liability
- *Monell v. Dept. of Social Services*, 436 U.S. 658 (1978) — policy vs. acts of employees
- *City of Oklahoma City v. Tuttle*, 471 U.S. 808 (1985) — single act不足以建立政策
- *Pembaur v. Cincinnati*, 475 U.S. 469 (1986) — single decision by policymaker = policy
- *Bd. of County Comm'rs v. Brown*, 520 U.S. 397 (1997) — causation required
- *Snyder v. Trepagnier*, 142 F.3d 791 (5th Cir. 1998) — deliberate indifference standard
- *Burge v. St. Tammany Parish*, 336 F.3d 363 (5th Cir. 2003) — custom/policy analysis

### Qualified Immunity
- *Safford v. Redding*, 557 U.S. 361 (2009) — qualified immunity analysis
- *Mullenix v. Luna*, 577 U.S. 7 (2015) — clearly established law standard
- *Kisela v. Hughes*, 138 S.Ct. 1148 (2018) — objective reasonableness

### Conspiracy
- *Mason v. Lafayette City-Parish Council*, 201 F.3d 1094 (9th Cir. 2000) — § 1983 civil conspiracy
- *Ward v. Kirkland*, 196 F.3d 1138 (10th Cir. 1999) — agreement + overt act
