# COURT_RULES — Applicable Procedural Rules

## Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (FRCP)

### FRCP 8 — Pleadings
- **Rule 8(a):** Notice pleading — claim for relief must contain: (1) jurisdiction, (2) grounds for relief, (3) demand for judgment
- **Rule 8(b):** Defenses — responsive pleading must admit or deny allegations
- **Rule 8(c):** Affirmative defenses — must be raised in responsive pleading or waived

### FRCP 9 — Pleading Special Matters
- **Rule 9(b):** Fraud, mistake, condition of mind — must state circumstances with particularity

### FRCP 10 — Form of Pleadings
- **Rule 10(b):** Each claim founded on separate transaction or occurrence must be stated in separate count

### FRCP 12 — Defenses
- **Rule 12(b)(6):** Failure to state a claim upon which relief can be granted
- **Rule 12(e):** More definite statement — when pleading is so vague that party cannot reasonably prepare response
- **Rule 12(f):** Motion to strike — strike insufficient defense or redundant/irrelevant matter
- **Rule 12(b) motion deadline:** 21 days after service of complaint, OR 21 days after service of motion under Rule 12(e)

### FRCP 15 — Amended Pleadings
- **Rule 15(a):** Amendment as of right before responsive pleading; thereafter by leave of court
- **Rule 15(a)(2):** Leave shall be freely given when justice so requires
- **Rule 15(d):** Supplemental pleadings — events occurring after pleading

### FRCP 26 — Discovery
- **Rule 26(a)(1):** Initial disclosures — 14 days after Rule 26(f) conference
- **Rule 26(a)(2):** Expert witness disclosures
- **Rule 26(b)(1):** Scope — relevant, non-privileged matter
- **Rule 26(c):** Protective orders

### FRCP 37 — Discovery Sanctions
- **Rule 37(a):** Motion to compel discovery
- **Rule 37(e):** Failure to preserve electronically stored information

### FRCP 56 — Summary Judgment
- **Rule 56(a):** Shall grant if no genuine dispute as to material fact and movant entitled to judgment
- **Rule 56(c):** Judgment shall be rendered promptly after expiration of time for response
- **Rule 56(d):** Continuance to obtain affidavits when facts are unavailable to movant

### FRCP 65 — Injunctive Relief
- **Rule 65(b):** Temporary restraining order — ex parte or with notice

### FRCP 71 — Service on Persons Not Parties
- **Rule 71:** May serve written request on non-party to admit facts or genuineness of documents

### FRCP 81 — Proceedings In Forma Pauperis
- **Rule 81(a)(6):** Prisoner § 1983 cases — PLRA requirements

## Local Rules — S.D. Tex.

### LR 7.1 — Motions
- Response due 21 days after service; reply due 7 days after response
- No oral argument unless court orders

### LR 7.2 — Proposed Orders
- Parties must submit proposed order in Word format via email to judge's chambers

### LR 7.3 — Length Limits
- Motions limited to 25 pages; responses 25 pages; replies 10 pages

### LR 26.1 — Discovery
- Initial disclosures required within 14 days of Rule 26(f) conference
- Discovery cutoff set by court in scheduling order

### LR 56.1 — Summary Judgment
- Separate statement of undisputed/material facts required
- Response must admit or deny each fact

### LR 79.2 — Exhibits
- All exhibits must be filed on CM/ECF with exhibit numbers

## Key Deadlines (when set)
- **Discovery deadline:** Set in scheduling order
- **Dispositive motions:** 30 days before trial date
- **Pretrial conference:** Per scheduling order
- **Trial:** Per court calendar
