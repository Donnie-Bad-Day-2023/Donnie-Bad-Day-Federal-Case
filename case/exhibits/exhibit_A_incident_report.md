## Key Evidentiary Contradictions

### 1. Simultaneous Timestamp
The report states that the "observation" of expired registration AND the activation of emergency lights occurred at the **exact same minute — 2220 hours.** No documented interval exists between observation and activation. This means Gonzales cannot establish that he verified the registration status before initiating the stop. Any lawful stop requires the officer to have observed the violation at the inception — not after the fact.

### 2. Vehicle Description Contradiction (Internal)
- **Assignment:** "2-Door Chrysler Sebring" (correct vehicle type)
- **Summary:** "said pickup" ← Factually impossible. Chrysler does not manufacture a Sebring pickup.
- **Exhibit B:** "2 DOOR SEDAN" ← Third distinct description

Two official documents (Incident Report + Sworn Affidavit) produce **three irreconcilable vehicle descriptions.** This internal contradiction proves that **no MDT (mobile data terminal) plate query was run** — any such query would have returned the correct, consistent vehicle description.

### 3. No Documented Plate Query
The contradictory descriptions demonstrate that Gonzales did not run a license plate check via MDT before initiating the stop. If he had, he would have received the correct vehicle description and would not have contradicted himself within the same document.

## Legal Significance
- Establishes the **Fourth Amendment seizure began with no documented reasonable suspicion at the inception**
- The simultaneous timestamp undermines the probable cause basis for the stop
- The vehicle description contradictions undermine the credibility of the entire factual foundation
- Proves the stop was not based on actual observation of the expired registration sticker
