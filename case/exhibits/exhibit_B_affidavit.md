# Exhibit B — Sworn Expired Registration Affidavit
## Thompson v. Matagorda County et al. — 3:25-cv-00253

---

## Document Summary
Defendant Deputy Raymond Gonzales's sworn affidavit asserting expired registration as the basis for the stop and arrest. Filed as the charging instrument. **Three fatal defects** independently destroy its legal validity.

---

## Three Fatal Defects

### Defect 1 — Impossible Vehicle Description
The affidavit describes the vehicle as a **"2004, WHITE IN COLOR, CHRYSLER, 2 DOOR, SEDAN."**
- A 2-door sedan is NOT a model Chrysler manufactured for the Sebring line
- The 2004 Chrysler Sebring was produced only as a **2-door coupe** and a **convertible** — neither is classified as a sedan
- This description is **physically impossible** — proves the affidavit was NOT prepared from direct observation or verified records

### Defect 2 — Private Property Location
The affidavit states the vehicle was operated **"at 507 AVENUE I"** — a private residential driveway, NOT a public street.
- Vehicle was stopped on Tina Phillips's private property
- Directly undermines the claim that the offense was committed on a public roadway in the officer's presence

### Defect 3 — Blank Probable Cause Signature Block
The judicial probable cause finding line is **completely blank**:
> *"Justice of the Peace, Pct. ___, Matagorda County, Texas, having read the above affidavit find that there is sufficient probable cause for said offense."*

- **No magistrate signed it**
- **No judicial review of probable cause was ever conducted**
- This makes the entire prosecution void for lack of lawful charging instrument

## Strategic Significance

### Timing — Document Printed 6/20/2023
This document was **printed by Defendant Riley on 6/20/2023** — just **two days before the tow hearing** — with the probable cause signature block still blank. This proves that **as of the date of the tow hearing (6/22/2023), no judicial review of probable cause had ever been performed.**

### Combined with Exhibit E (Register of Actions)
- Exhibit E shows the case was **created on 5/16/2023** — the day after plaintiff's physical release from custody
- Combined with the blank probable cause signature block, this establishes the **entire prosecution was fabricated after the fact**

### Vehicle Description — Third Contradiction
This is the **third vehicle description across two documents**:
1. Incident Report: "2004, Chrysler, Sebring, 2-Door" (no body style)
2. Affidavit (Exhibit B): "2004, White, Chrysler, **2 DOOR SEDAN**" (impossible)
3. Booking/other docs: "2 door SLT" (different still)

Three different descriptions across two documents. **No MDT query was run. No verified observation occurred before the stop.**
