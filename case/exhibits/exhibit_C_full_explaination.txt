Strategic Significance

The booking sheet was printed by Defendant Riley on 6/20/2023 — 36 days after release and just before the tow hearing — with the

Judge, Court, Court Date, and Disposition fields all blank. This confirms that no judicial process had been

completed as of that date, which directly contradicts the Register of Actions (Exhibit E) showing a conviction

entered on 5/16/2023.

The 2-minute window between lights (2220) and custody (2222) is physically insufficient for Gonzales to have:

approached the vehicle, identified the driver, explained the reason for the stop, observed "verbal aggression,"

directed Thompson to exit, yanked him from the vehicle (door handle was broken — could only be opened

from outside), searched him for weapons, and placed him in custody. The timeline alone establishes that the

arrest decision was made before the stop.
