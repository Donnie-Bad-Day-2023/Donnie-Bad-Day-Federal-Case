--- CRITICAL NOTED DISCREPANCY ---
The affidavit states registration expired JANUARY 2022.
The traffic stop occurred MAY 14, 2023 — over 15 months later.
This is the probable cause document for the stop. The expired registration
from January 2022 was not the basis for a valid stop on May 14, 2023.
