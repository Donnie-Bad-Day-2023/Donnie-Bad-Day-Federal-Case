COMES NOW Plaintiff Donald Lynn Thompson, Jr., appearing pro se and sui 
juris, and respectfully files this Notice of Total Substitution to provide the Court 
and the Defendants with a clear, concise guide regarding the structural 
transformation of the First Amended Complaint.  
1. On May 25, 2026, pursuant to this Court's Order (Dkt. 44) granting leave 
to amend, Plaintiff timely filed his First Amended Complaint. At the time 
of filing, Plaintiff, as a pro se litigant, lacked an understanding of the local 
standing orders requiring a traditional side-by-side visual redline.

2. Plaintiff acts immediately and proactively to provide this notice to ensure 
absolute compliance to prevent any technical or procedural delays before 
the defense introduces a responsive motion.  
3. Because the First Amended Complaint is a complete factual, evidentiary, 
and structural rewrite of the initial pleading, a traditional "track-changes" 
or "redline" document would consist entirely of all struck-through text 
followed by entirely underlined text. Such a document would offer no 
practical clarity to the Court.  
4. Plaintiff explicitly advises the Court and the Defendants that the 
underlying reason and core constitutional grievance of this lawsuit have 
not changed.  
5. Rather than changing the core claim, the entire structure of the complaint 
has been completely overhauled to satisfy the strict factual pleading 
requirements mandated in this Court’s Order (Dkt. 44).  
In compliance with the spirit of the court's rules, the structural changes are 
defined as follows:  
● Factual Restructuring: General legal conclusions regarding the lack 
of probable cause have been entirely removed. They are replaced 
with precise physical, geometric, and temporal facts demonstrating

● Evidentiary Integration: To answer the Court's directive regarding
