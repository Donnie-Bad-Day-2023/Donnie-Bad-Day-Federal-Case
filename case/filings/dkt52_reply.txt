[J] XIV. DEFENDANTS' CONCESSION THAT THE OFFICER "DID NOT RUN THE PLATE" CONFIRMS A TOTAL LACK OF PRE-SEIZURE PROBABLE CAUSE AS A MATTER OF LAW

In an extraordinary misapplication of Fourth Amendment jurisprudence, Defendants explicitly argue that "Officer Gonzales was not required to run Plaintiff's license plate because he observed the expired registration status from the sticker" and seek to use Plaintiff's subsequent 2026 pleading admissions to validate a 2023 seizure (Dkt. 52, p. 4).

First, it is an unyielding tenet of constitutional law that the legality of a seizure must be evaluated solely based on the objective facts known to the officer at the exact moment the seizure is initiated. An admission made by a civil plaintiff years after the fact cannot travel backward through time to retroactively provide an officer with a legal foundation at 22:20 on the night of the stop.

Second, by explicitly conceding that Officer Gonzales did not run Plaintiff's license plate prior to or during the stop (Dkt. 52, p. 4), Defendants have effectively confessed to a severe Fourth Amendment violation.

An unverified visual observation of a windshield sticker is not a confirmed criminal offense until it is electronically or physically verified.

By admitting Gonzales "was not required to run the plate," Defendants admit the officer escalated a brief, unverified Class C misdemeanor traffic stop into a full, prolonged custodial over-detention and vehicle seizure without ever verifying the legal status of the vehicle.

[Kl XV. THE MATAGORDA COUNTY DOCUMENTS FEATURE FOUR DISTINCT VEHICLE DISCREPANCIES, PROVING A SYSTEMIC CUSTOM OF POST-HOC DATA FABRICATION

Defendants attempt to isolate Officer Gonzales's misidentification as a "simple misclassification" that does not negate probable cause (Dkt. 52, p. 4).

Plaintiff does not allege a mere isolated clerical typo; Plaintiff has pled a pervasive, multi-agency pattern of structural data discrepancy:

1. Deputy Gonzales's Incident Report (Exhibit A): "said pickup"
2. Sheriff's Office internal booking infrastructure: completely separate automotive class
3. Administrative records for County Attorney's civil tow hearing: two additional distinct physical mutations

This chaotic, four-way structural discrepancy is the direct electronic footprint of a "blind booking" system operating completely detached from real-time state registry verification.
