## CLAIMS (5 Counts)

1. **Count I - Unlawful Arrest/Detention** (§ 1983) - Against Gonzales and Sanders
   - TX Transp. Code § 543.003 requires citation, not arrest, for fine-only offense
   - § 502.407 provides remedial framework (dismissal upon compliance, max $20)
   
2. **Count II - Due Process/Brady Violations** (§ 1983) - Against Chau, Riley, Finlay, Sanders
   - Concealed bodycam/dashcam footage
   - Denied public records despite indigency
   
3. **Count III - Malicious Prosecution** (§ 1983) - Against Chau, Sanders, Finlay
   - Non-criminal offense used to fabricate criminal record
   
4. **Count IV - Civil Conspiracy** (§ 1985(3)) - Against all defendants
   - Coordinated suppression of evidence
   
5. **Count V - Monell Liability** (§ 1983) - Against Matagorda County
   - Unconstitutional policies/customs

## MAGISTRATE R&R (Dkt 42) - KEY FAILURES

The R&R was issued by Magistrate Judge Andrew M. Edison on April 27, 2026 and recommends:
- Granting defense MTD (dismissing all claims)
- Sua sponte dismissing Jackson Towing
- Giving Plaintiff 14 days to amend

### Critical Problems with R&R:

1. **Fails to address § 502.407 argument** - Plaintiff's central statutory argument received only a footnote; R&R analyzed § 543.004 (defense statute) instead

2. **Adopted defense MTD wholesale** - R&R mirrors defense arguments without independent analysis

3. **Conflated traffic stop with custodial arrest** - Treated probable cause for stop as validating arrest

4. **Qualified immunity analysis assumed conclusion** - "even if Thompson's theory is correct (it is not)" - can't assume the answer

5. **Judicial immunity analysis incomplete** - Didn't address § 502.407 connection; ignored jurisdictional defect argument

6. **Didn't address Brady/Count II** - No separate analysis of due process/Brady claims

7. **Monell analysis is circular** - Defeats Monell by assuming arrest/magistration were lawful (the very questions in dispute)

8. **Rooker-Feldman misapplied** - Plaintiff brings independent § 1983 claims, not appeal of state court

9. **Sua sponte dismissal of Jackson** - No notice, no opportunity to amend

10. **Ignored Count IV (Conspiracy)** - No analysis of § 1985(3) claim
