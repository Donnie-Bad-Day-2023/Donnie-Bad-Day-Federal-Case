# Brady Evidence — 3:25-cv-00253

## Brady Framework
Brady v. Maryland, 373 U.S. 83 (1963): The prosecution must disclose all favorable evidence — both exculpatory and impeachment — to the defense. Suppression of such evidence by police, prosecutors, or agents of the state violates due process.
