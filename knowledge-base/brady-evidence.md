# Brady Evidence — 3:25-cv-00253

## Brady Framework
Under Brady v. Maryland, 373 U.S. 83 (1963), the prosecution must disclose all favorable evidence — both exculpatory and impeachment — to the defense. Suppression of such evidence violates due process.
