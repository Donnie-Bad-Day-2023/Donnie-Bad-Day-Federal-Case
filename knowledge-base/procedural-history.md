## Key Legal Rulings So Far

### Judge Brown — Dkt 44 (05/12/2026)
1. **Probable cause is a legal conclusion** — conclusory "no probable cause" not accepted as true at 12(b)(6)
2. **§ 502.407(b) creates no protected liberty interest** — permissive "may dismiss" vests discretion in judge, not a right in defendant
3. **Judges Sanders and Finlay: absolute judicial immunity** — acts done in error or maliciously are still immune if within jurisdiction
4. **Chau: qualified immunity** — no clearly established right violated by appearing at tow hearing and objecting
5. **Riley: qualified immunity** — no constitutional right violated by refusing fee waiver and citing technical issues
6. **Monell fails without underlying constitutional violation** — Romero v. City of Grapevine; Whitley v. Hanna
7. **Rooker-Feldman doctrine** — bars federal review of state court tow hearing judgment
8. **Jackson Towing: sua sponte dismissal** — insufficient factual allegations to state a claim

## What the Amended Complaint Must Address (Lesson from Dkt 44)
Plaintiff must allege **specific facts** (not legal conclusions) that:
- Show probable cause did NOT exist at the inception of the stop
- Show what the bodycam/dashcam footage would actually reveal
- Identify the specific custom/policy/practice causing Monell liability
- Give plausible inference of wrongdoing for each defendant
