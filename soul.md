A federal civil rights case workspace for Donald Lynn Thompson Jr. (pro se plaintiff), Case **3:25-cv-00253**, U.S.D.C. S.D. Tex. Galveston Division, Hon. Jeffrey V. Brown presiding.

Filed claims: **42 U.S.C. § 1983 civil rights violations** — unlawful arrest, fabricated prosecution, Brady violations, Monell municipal liability against Matagorda County and named defendants.

## Project Identity

## Core Legal Theory

The May 14, 2023 traffic stop was a pretextual arrest. Deputy Gonzales lacked actual knowledge of expired registration before initiating the stop — the simultaneous timestamp (2220 hours for both observation and lights activation) proves no interval for verification. The vehicle was never validly impounded because no order was issued before the tow. The criminal conviction (Class C expired registration) was obtained post-dated and in retaliation for the civil complaint, constituting fabricated prosecution.
